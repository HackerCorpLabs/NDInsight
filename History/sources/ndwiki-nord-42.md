> "The NORD-42 is an **OEM version of the NORD-12** system. It was manufactured
> for Noratom-Norcontrol A/S."

So the NORD-42 is not a separate design; it is a rebadged NORD-12 built for one
customer. That customer matters: **Norcontrol** is the same company that took the
very first NORD-1, serial 2, for the anti-collision system on M/S Taimyr.

Norcontrol used it as the central processor in their **DATABRIDGE** system, and
it also went into ship simulators at maritime schools in Norway and abroad,
including China and Mexico.

Its three cited sources are all outside our reach: a Norcontrol SERVICE HANDBOOK,
a chapter of *Handbuch fuer die Schiffsfuehrung*, and page 16 of *Ingenioeren*
number 45, November 1980.

The NORD-42 is an OEM version of the NORD-12 system. It was manufactured for Noratom-Norcontrol A/S.[1]

Norcontrol used the NORD-42 as the central processor in their DATABRIDGE system. [2] It was also used in simulators on a number of maritime schools both in Norway and other places as China and Mexico.[3]
