**Status: SECONDARY on paper, EYEWITNESS in practice - and unlike anything else we
have.** It is a table of every company name the business carried from 1967 to
2004, with a running commentary by Jonny Oddene, who joined Norsk Data on 13 May
1986 and stayed through the whole unwinding. From 1986 on, it is first-hand.

**It is also furious, and openly so.** The commentary is full of contempt for
"slipsfolket" (the tie-people, the suits) and "blaarussen" (business-school
types), for consultants, for seminars, and for renamings decided after a heavy
night out. Read it as testimony from inside, not as neutral history - but
testimony from inside is exactly what none of our other sources have.

## What it settles

**The founding dates.** Two entries, not one, which is why our sources disagreed:

- **1967-07-07** - NORDATA - Norsk Data Elektronikk, a *personal* company
- **1967-09-19** - Norsk Data Elektronikk AS, converted to a limited company

That matches the norsk-data.com timeline exactly and confirms 7 July as the
founding. English Wikipedia's "August 8, 1967" matches neither date.

**The rename to ND**: 1976-01-01, Norsk Data AS.

## What it adds - the Norwegian afterlife, which no other source has

English Wikipedia follows the British tail (Telenor keeps the ND name, buys CIX
and XTML, sells to 2e2 in 2003). This page follows the Norwegian one, and it is a
different and longer chain:

Details worth keeping, all from the commentary:

- **1991**: the business was broken into small companies, everything named "-Team"
  and "-Partner", "because with names like that you cannot fail - so the suits
  think". An attempt was made to kill off Sintran, including an article in the
  customer newspaper.
- **1992**: renamed **Comma** because the bosses got dizzy looking at the ND dots.
  The new name was unveiled at a party at Smuget while people taped Comma stickers
  over every ND logo.
- **1995**: **Telenor Bedrift** bought them. The account says Telenor needed
  anything it could find to dress up its own accounts, and that the company by then
  carried a huge loan, having gone from rich to owing money with the buildings sold.
- **2000**: moved out of the Telenor group (held under 50%) so Telenor could float
  clean - "Telenor is rid of all the dead meat and is ready for the stock exchange".
- **2001**: the ND department sat at the Skullerud head office while the rest were
  put in a back yard at Hasle. Phone and data lines were cut more than once for
  non-payment, and ID cards stopped working. **The ND department was then closed
  abruptly**, to the surprise of staff and customers alike - "stakkars ND", poor
  ND. A closing gathering was held with part of the hard core.
- **2003**: the Skullerud head office shut and **everything in the store and
  workshop was thrown out** - "it was only some old rubbish". The timeline records
  this under 2003; here it is from the inside.
- **2004**: **Sintran Data** keeps the last of it alive so remaining customers can
  still be served. "We are not a top-heavy organisation, in fact only workers -
  long live the ND spirit."

## Why this matters

Two things. First, the company did not simply stop in 1992 - the same people and
the same service business ran under nine more names for another twelve years, and
in one form still exist. Second, this page's folder is the source of most of the
photographs in `History/images/`: `history/` also holds `nord-1.jpg`,
`nord-5.jpg`, `nord-10.jpg`, `nord-10-50.jpg`, `nord-100.jpg`, `nd-100.jpg`,
