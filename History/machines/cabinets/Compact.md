# Compact cabinet

*The small ND-100-line cabinet. The longest-lived ND product of all.*

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Used from** | mid-1980s |
| **Type** | Cabinet |
| **Family** | [ND-1xx standalone](../cpu/nd-1xx/) |
| **Takes which CPU** | Any one [ND-1xx CPU board](../cpu/nd-1xx/) |
| **Coprocessor?** | No - see [5000 Compact](Compact-5000-COMSON.md) |

## The short version

A much smaller box for customers who did not need a full rack. Like the large
cabinet it takes whichever 1xx CPU board you bought, which is why ND could sell an
"ND-100 Compact" and later an "ND-110 Compact" without changing the cabinet.

It has the distinction of carrying the **last Norsk Data machine ever delivered**:
a model 12 went to a customer in August 2001, nine years after the company had
ceased to exist.

## Specification

| Item | Value |

## What goes in it

| Slot / backplane | Holds |
|---|---|
