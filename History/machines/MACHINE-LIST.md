# The list of surviving machines

*266 individual Norsk Data machines, by serial number, from ND's own tracking
list.*

## What this is, and what it is not

The sintran.com archive carries a table of individual machines - serial number,
model, name, owner, location and status. It is a **service and installed-base
register**, kept by people who maintained these machines, not a survivors list.

**Read the status column carefully:**

| Status | Machines | What it means |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| Unknown | 222 | Whereabouts or condition not known - **not** "still running" |
| Dismantled | 41 | Confirmed scrapped |
| Saved | 2 | Confirmed preserved |
| Restoration | 1 | Being restored |

So the honest summary is: **266 machines are known by serial number, 2 are
confirmed saved, 41 are confirmed gone, and the rest are simply unaccounted for.**

## Machines with names

**130 of the 266 have names.** ND and its customers named machines the way people
name boats - Norse gods, places, characters, jokes:

- **Norse gods**: THOR, ODIN, BALDER, KOLOSS
- **Places** (often the site itself): RYGGE, KJEVIK, BODOE, Sauda Trafo, Hylen, Hjorteland Trafo, Blaalli Trafo
- **ND's own machines**: Obelix, Hades, Rosa and Luring were the operations machines; COMSON-A and COMSON-B the disc test pair
- **Others**: MAGDA, RALLAR, D-04, WP1

`COMSON-A` (serial 21175) and `COMSON-B` (21176) are the reason the ND-100/5000
Compact drawing set is titled "COMSON" - the drawings were named after ND's own
test machines, not the other way round.

## Caveats

- This is **one archive's register**, not a manufacturer's production record. It
  cannot tell you how many were built.
- "Unknown" is the commonest status by far and means exactly that.
- Serial numbers are not continuous, and some records have none.
- Type numbers appear that no manual here explains **[?]**.
