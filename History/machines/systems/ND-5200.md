## The short version

One of the ND-5000 models. On this line the model number is largely a
**configuration**, not a different design: the CPU type, the master clock setting
and which caches are enabled are what separate one model from the next. The ND-5200 is the entry model: CPU type 1 has no cache layer and no AAP, so **floating-point operations are performed by the microprogram** rather than by hardware.
