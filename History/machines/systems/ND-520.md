## The short version

One of the three model numbers the [ND-500/1](../cpu/nd-5xx/ND-500-1.md) was sold under. ND
split the ND-500 into ND-520, ND-560 and ND-570 in 1982. Little else is recorded
about this model specifically - it does not appear on ND's later performance
ladder, which starts at the ND-510/CX.
