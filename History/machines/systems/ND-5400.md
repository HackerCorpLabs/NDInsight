## The short version

One of the ND-5000 models. On this line the model number is largely a
**configuration**, not a different design: the CPU type, the master clock setting
and which caches are enabled are what separate one model from the next. Note the clock: the ND-5400 runs *slower* than the cheaper ND-5200, and gets its performance from caching instead.
