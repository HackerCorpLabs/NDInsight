## The short version

The last processor Norsk Data sold, and one of the longest-lived: ND-5850
systems were still being upgraded and delivered to Norsk Tipping, NSB, Statskraft
and Stavanger Aftenblad in 1998, and a new satellite went to a customer in
February 2000 - eight years after the company itself had ended.

Sources disagree on when it arrived: both Wikipedias say 1987, the norsk-data.com
timeline says 1990. The Dolphin chips in it are dated 1989.

## What was new

- The end of the line - the last ND processor, built by the spin-off company.
- Like the [ND-5830](ND-5830.md), it is an **improved Samson** rather than a new machine: same functionality, fewer boards, better ASICs.
