## The short version

One of the model numbers the ND-500 architecture was sold under. Model
numbers on this line indicate **performance and user capacity**, not different
architectures - the underlying CPU is the [ND-500/2](../cpu/nd-5xx/ND-500-2.md) implementation. ND rated the range on
its own ADP/OA scale, where an ND-100/CX is 1.0, and in Whetstone MIPS.
