# Sales-Info — Norsk Data Sales Information Documents

**OCR'd Norsk Data "Sales Information Document" sheets** (document series `ND-SIDnnn-<rev>-<lang>`).

These are the internal, company-confidential sales sheets ND wrote for its own sales
force: a product's positioning, configuration/upgrade paths, structure lists (the exact
article numbers a salesman had to quote), and pricing-relevant options.

They are kept apart from [Product-Info/](../Product-Info/README.md) — those are the
outward-facing product data sheets/brochures handed to customers — and from
[Installation-Description/](../Installation-Description/README.md), which holds the
formal "Program Description" install/requirements forms.
