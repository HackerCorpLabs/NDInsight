# ND 218 Graphics Colour Plotter

![Norsk Data Logo](ascii_art_placeholder_for_logo)

## Introduction

The ND 218 graphic colour plotter is ideally suited to provide print-outs of quality colour graphics from the NOTIS-BG program. The colour plotter is designed for use with two paper sizes; A4 and A3. The ND 218 is Norsk Data's version of the Hewlett Packard 7475 plotter.

Even the most complicated data is easy to understand and to explain when presented as a multi-colour graph or chart. The ND 218 is easy to operate and very flexible for this use.

## Features

- Well-suited for plotting of:
  - graphs
  - pie charts
  - bar charts
  - schematics
  - engineering drawings
- Two paper sizes: A4 and A3
- Choose any six colours
- Printing on paper or overhead transparency film
- Easy-to-load, 6-pen carousel
- Built-in automatic pen capping and damping
- Easy to operate

## Product Description

### Media

Paper or overhead transparency film.

### Media Sizes

| Size           | Dimensions                    |
|----------------|-------------------------------|
| ISO A4         | 210x297 mm (8.5x11 in. ANSI A)|
| ISO A3         | 297x420 mm (11x17 in. ANSI B) |

### Resolution

Smallest addressable step size: 0.025 mm (0.001 in.)

### Pens

- Number: 6, automatic pen changing
- Type: fiber tip

### Character Sets

19-ANSI ASCII, English, German, French, Norwegian, Danish, Swedish

### Power Requirements

- Source: 100, 120, 220, 240V -10% + 5%
- Frequency: 48-66 Hz
- Consumption: 35 W maximum

### Environmental Range

- Operating: 0°C to 55°C
- Non-operating: -40°C to 75°C

### Size

| Dimension  | Measurement          |
|------------|----------------------|
| Height     | 127 mm (5 in.)       |
| Width      | 568 mm (22.4 in.)    |
| Depth      | 367 mm (14.5 in.)    |

### Weight

- Net: 7 kg (16.0 lb.)
- Shipping: Approx. 11 kg (25.0 lb.)

```
    +-------------------+
    |      ND 100/500   |
    |                   |
    |      Computer     |
    +-------------------+

    |       Plotter     |
    +-------------------+
```
