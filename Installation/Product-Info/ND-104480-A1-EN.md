# Multifunction Printer

## INTRODUCTION

The ND 104480 printer is a correspondence-quality printer which may also be used to produce high-speed draft quality output. The ND 104480 printer also includes an acoustic housing which reduces the noise of the printer to an acceptable level.

## FEATURES

- A NOTIS Office Printer
- Correspondence quality at 80 characters per second
- Double hopper sheet-feeder with manual insertion chute as option
- Tractor-feed option
- Acoustic cover
- Draft quality at 300 characters per second
- 5 standard fonts (type styles)
- Semi-graphic character set for NOTIS
- English, French, German, Norwegian, Swedish and Danish options
- Horizontal pitch 10, 12 and 15 characters per inch
- Wide selection of loadable fonts
- Cassette ribbon
- Optional printer stand
- Switchable 110/220/240 Volt ac., 50/60 Hz

## THE SHEET-FEEDER

The double sheet-feeder consists of 2 hoppers for 200 sheets each, one stacker for printed paper and a chute for manual insertion of single sheets and multipart forms.

NOTIS itself will handle the sheet-feeder by allowing the user to issue directives ordering the printer to take sheets from the main or the alternative hopper.
