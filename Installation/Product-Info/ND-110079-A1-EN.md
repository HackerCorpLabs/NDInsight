# INKJET PRINTER, PT88S

The ND110079/ND110080 PT88S InkJet printer is intended as a personal printer connected to an ND display terminal. The InkJet printing mechanism is designed for super-silent operation and a higher printing speed than usually found on small, personal printers. It prints a limited ND-NOTIS character set, including simulated box-graphics.

The PT88S is the ideal personal printer for those who require a printer that offers a better functionality than is usually found on small, personal printers. It prints in both draft and "near-letter-quality" (NLQ) modes, it is super silent, and it prints with 200 cps in draft mode and 66 cps in NLQ mode. The approximate throughput is 38 seconds per page at 1500 characters per page.

It may be used with all ND-NOTIS Office Support products, including NOTIS-BUSINESS-GRAPHICS. It cannot be used with NOTIS- DRAW at the present time.

The ND110079/ND110080 InkJet printer requires "InkJet paper" for optimal result, but it also prints with acceptable results on ordinary copying paper. The contents of the ink cartridge can print an average of 5 million characters.

## The Main Features at a Glance

- InkJet printing mechanism
- Super-silent operation, 200 cps < 45 dB (A)
- Optimised, bidirectional printing
- 9 x 9 dots in draft mode, 9 x 18 dots  
  in Near-Letter-Quality (NLQ)
- Paper-feed system with tractor and friction drive
- Paper width up to 250 mm
- Graphics printing (bit-image, 8 bits),  
  prints output from NOTIS-BUSINESS-GRAPHICS
- Horizontal and vertical tabulation
- Self-test programs

## Operating Principle

A cross-sectional drawing of the InkJet print head is shown below. It operates according to the "drop-on-demand" principle. The ink is contained in an easily replaceable ink cartridge. The ink level is controlled via electrodes, which flash the "alarm" lamp on the control panel and signal the interface.

The ink flows from the cartridge into a central supply channel before being distributed to the nine jet channels, which are closed off by a nozzle array plate with nine nozzles.

When a voltage pulse is applied to the piezotube, the pressure in the jet channel is briefly increased and a drop is ejected from the nozzle. A seal automatically closes off the nozzles during lengthy printing intervals and when the power is switched off.

# INKJET PRINTER, PT88S

## Technical Specifications

### Printing Method
InkJet dot matrix, drop-on-demand

### Character Matrix Format
- 9 x 9 dots in draft mode
- 9 x 18 dots in NLQ mode

| Printing speed (cps) | Draft mode | NLQ mode |
|----------------------|------------|----------|
| at 1/6" line feed    | 10 12 15 17 | 10 12 15 17 cpi |
| at 20 char per line  | 66 77 74 74 | 26 28 31 31  |
| at 40 char per line  | 99 108 116 119 | 38 42 47 47  |
| at 60 char per line  | 119 133 149 149 | 44 50 57 57  |
| at 80 char per line  | 133 149 171 171 | 48 55 64 64  |
| at 136 char per line | 154 182 207 207 | 52 65 75 75  |

### Additional Specifications

- **Printing Direction:** Bidirectional, logic seeking
- **Paper Width:** 124 - 250 mm fanfold, 105 - 216 mm single sheet
- **Paper Type:** InkJet paper
- **Paper Weight:** 60 - 100 g/m2
- **Number of Plies:** 1 original
- **Noise Level:** < 45 dB (A)
- **Mean Time Between Failure:** 5000 hours power ON, 1500 hours printing

### Ink Supply
- Approx. 5 mill. characters
- Approx. 3000 A4 pages
- Disposable cartridge, easily exchanged

### Ink
- Black, smear and light proof, indelible ink

### Interface
- **ND110079:** RS-232-C (V24)
- **ND110080:** RS-422 (V11)

### Data Rate
110 - 9600 bits/s

### Environmental Specifications

- **Operating Temperature:** 10 to 40 degrees C
- **Relative Humidity:** 10 to 90%, non-condensing
- **Absolute Humidity:** 25 g/m3 max
- **Operating Air Pressure:** > 600 mbar (max. 3500 m above sea level)
- **Operating Position:** Any angle up to 7 degrees

### Electrical Specifications

- **Voltage:** 220/240 VAC +-10%-15%, 50-60 Hz +-5%; 117/127 VAC +-10%-15%, 60 Hz +-5%
- **Power Consumption:** < 20 W stand by, < 30 W operating
- **Dimensions:** Height 140 mm, Width 410 mm, Depth 310 mm
- **Weight:** 7.5 kg
- **Electrical Safety:** Protection class II, DIN IEC 380/VDE 0806, UL 478 and CSA C22.2 No 154-M, 1983

### RF Suppression
Limit value category B (N-12dB) DIN 57871 / VDE 871, conforming to OBR no 1115/1982 and FCC Rules Part 15, Subpart J.
