# ND LINE-PRINTER 815 BPA

## Introduction

The ND LINE-PRINTER 815 BPA features easily interchangeable font bands of 64 or (optional) 96 characters. The maximum print throughput is 1200 lines per minute using the 64-character band. It uses continuous, fanfold, pin-feed forms in widths from 3 to 17 inches and lengths from 3 to 22 inches.

## Features

- High speed printing
- Excellent print quality on up to six-part forms
- Microprocessor-based auto-diagnosis and off-line testing
- Simple type-belt exchange
- Easy replacement of paper and ink ribbon
- Form-length selectable from front panel
- Powered stacker included as standard
- Optional long-line adaptor for remote installation.

## Product Specification

The ND LINE-PRINTER 815 BPA combines proven printer design and modern technology to provide a high-quality printer which is easy to operate and maintain.

- **Printing speed:**
  - With 64-character set: 1200 lines/min.
  - With 96-character set: 880 lines/min.

- **Printing technique:** type-belt impact  
  Software control of paper movements  
  (form feed control)

- **Ribbon:** 30 mm x 100 m (1.2 in. x 328 ft) continuous loop

- **Number of columns:** 80, 132 or 136, selectable

- **Horizontal spacing:** 2.54 mm (10 CPI)

- **Vertical spacing:** 4.23 mm (6 LPI) or 3.18 mm (8 LPI), selectable
- **Line advance time:** 14 ms for single line advance
- **Slew speed:** 42.4 cm/s (16.7 ''/s)
- **Line counter:** included as standard
- **Form width:** 76.2 mm to 431.8 mm (3 to 17 '')
- **Form length:** 76.2 mm to 558.8 mm (3 to 22 '')
- **Copies:** max. 6 (including original)
- **Standard bands:**
  - Norwegian/Danish
  - Swedish/Finnish
  - UK
  - US ASCII 
  - All have OCR-B font style
- **Interface:** Parallel Data Product
- **Controller:** ND 106560, Plotter/Printer DMA controller.
- **Optional long-line adaptor:** ND 110384 for remote installation up to 100 meters apart. NB! The controller is the same.

## Physical Dimensions

| Specification | Measurement | 
|---------------|-------------|
| Height        | 1,095 mm (43.1 '') |
| Width         | 700 mm (27.6 '')   |
| Depth         | 1,050 mm (41.3 '') |
| Weight        | 220 kg (485 lb)    |

- **Power source:** Selectable 100/110/115/120/200/208/215/220/240 V AC ±10% 50/60Hz ±2.4% single phase
- **Power consumption:** maximum 1.0 kVA 64-character set
- **Acoustic noise:** 55dBA (ECMA Standard Sound Pressure level)

## Environmental Specifications

- **Ambient temperature**
  - Operating: 5° C to 40° C (41° F to 104° F)
  - Not operating: -20° C to 50° C (-4° F to 122° F)
  - Gradient: 5° C/h (27° F/h) or below
  
- **Relative humidity**
  - Operating: 20% to 80%
  - Not operating: 10% to 90% (non condensing)
  - Gradient: 30%/day or less

- **MTBF:** 4,000 hours
- **MTTR:** 30 minutes
