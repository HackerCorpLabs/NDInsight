# ND 846 Device Switch for Magnetic Tape

## INTRODUCTION

The ND 846 is a free-standing unit which provides for the transfer of magnetic tape or line printer from one CPU to another. This produces a greater degree of efficiency and utilization of the device and makes hook-up of cables unnecessary. Thus the operator's functions are made simpler and easier to perform.

## FEATURES

- Direct connection to two separate CPU's
- Easy operation
- No power consumption
- No maintenance

## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

The Device Switch is connected to the two CPU's by standard cables.

It is important to note that the system must be stopped before switching can be performed.
