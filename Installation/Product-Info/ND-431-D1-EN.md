## INTRODUCTION

The ND 431 band printer offers a wide variety of easily interchanged, horizontal moving character sets. The ND 431 prints 64 characters at 600 lpm.

The ND 431 also offers optionally increased print density as a source for significant savings in paper costs. Standard density is 10 cpi. The economical option is 15.

## FEATURES

- Character set sizes: 64 and 96
- Operator changeable character sets
- Self-adjusting character set size
- Self-adjusting 10 or 15 cpi
- Designed for low-grade or recycled paper
- Requires only 0.5 square meter to floor space
- Automatic motor turnoff after 30 sec. without printing
- Single to six-part forms, 4” to 16 3/4” wide
- Meets international safety standards
- Fuseless hammer-fault detection with LED indicators
- LSI hammer-driver logic
- Paper motion monitoring

## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

### Operator Controls and Adjustments

To make operation of the printer as easy as possible, the number of controls and adjustments has been kept to a minimum. Two pushbuttons, (START/STOP and FORM FEED) are located on the top front of the cabinet adjacent to the window. Pressing START resets all logic circuits, enabling printing to start when data is received. Pressing STOP interrupts printing.

### Print Mechanism

Each print hammer is activated by its magnetic actuator through a push rod. This rod enables the actuators to be spread farther from each other to isolate them mechanically and magnetically. Since the rod also enables only the hammers themselves to be shifted for sharing, a relatively low force is required to shift the hammers between positions. This force is obtained from a servo.
