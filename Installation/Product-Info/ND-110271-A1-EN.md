# ND COLOUR HARDCOPY

## Introduction

NORSK DATA offers complete solutions to the manufacturing industry in the CAD/CAM/CIM application area through the TECHNOVISION product family. The wide variety of software products is available on a platform of powerful 32-bit minicomputers, workstations and other related peripherals.

The ND Colour Hardcopy 720 offers the latest advances in colour ink-jet technology to produce high contrast copies in seven colours, fast.

The ND Colour Hardcopy 720 is a small but vital addition to the range of peripherals specially designed for use with Technovision CAD/CAM systems; the result of careful analysis of the CAD engineer's need for a quiet, reliable and economic unit.

Varying market needs and the different Technovision applications and customers have led to the production of a range of CAD/CAM workstations. The ND Colour Hardcopy 720 is a multipurpose unit for use with all ND CAD workstations.

## Page 3

# ND Colour Hardcopy

## Features

- Advanced colour ink-jet technology.
- 120 dots/inch resolution.
- Seven colours: Black, Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Violet, Blue and Green from four primary coloured inks.
- Fast copy time: 2 - 3.5 minutes dependent on CAD workstation type.
- Easy maintenance requires only paper and easily replaceable ink cartridges.
- Economic to install and operate.

## Interfaces

The unit can be connected to any Norsk Data Technovision workstation up to a maximum of 6 m distance via standard Centronics interface.

## Accessories

- Ink cartridges set of 4
- Paper roll

## Prerequisites

Specify following firmware kits:
- ND 701050 with ND9791/9752 Workstation Colour (new model)
- ND 700144 with ND9762 CAD Workstation Colour (old model)
