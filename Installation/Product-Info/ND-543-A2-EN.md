## Introduction

The ND 543 - Magnetic tape drive, the ND 537 Magnetic Tape Formatter, and the ND 560 - Magnetic Tape Controller constitute one of the leading minicomputer tape systems in the market. The formatter and drive are delivered by Storage Technology Corporation, a well reputed producer of computer peripherals.

## Features

- Up to four drives on each controller
- May be upgraded to 3 densities, 800-1600-6250 bpi  
  (Upgrade kit for magtape drive: ND 549, upgrade  
  kit for magtape controller: ND 549)
- Speed 125 ips
- In a multiple drive system, the formatter can  
  handle a mixture of densities
- Automatic thread/load for both open reels and  
  easy-load cartridges
- Single vacuum assisted capstan
- Dual vacuum columns
- Dual gap READ/WRITE head and full width ERASE head
- Quick release, positive action file reel hub
- Vacuum assisted tape cleaner
- Microprogram controlled tape unit sequencing  
  and, diagnostics
- Resident maintenance controls
- Power window - automatic closing and opening  
  on load and unload operations
- One drive may rewind without blocking the others  
  on the same controller

## Product Description

A two-phase digital tachometer in the capstan motor provides precise tape positioning and stop-lock control, at an accuracy of 0.00565 inches.

For speed control, tach pulses are compared to a crystal clock pulse.

In addition to automatic thread/load, extraordinarily accurate tape loading is assured by a minimum length, straightshot, gravity-assisted tape path. Tape acceleration is kept to a minimum by a microsequencer-controlled liner reel servo system.

Tape drag and tape edge damage is minimized by the use of hydrostatic air bearings at all critical points. A hydrostatic rewind foot prevents tape and head wear during high speed rewind. And a unique tape footage counter, in conjunction with the digital tack and microsequencer, smooths tape acceleration and deceleration for faster and gentler rewind.

# Specifications

## Tape Format

ANSI Compatibility.

Read and write as specified by ANSI X3.39-1973 (PE) and ANSI x3.54-1976-(GCR).

### Tape Motion Performance

- **Rated velocity**: 125 inches per second nominal

### Magnetic Tape Media

- Should conform to ANSI X3.40-1976, i.e. 1.5 milli inch thick tape. 2400' on a 10 1/2" reel.
- **Velocity variation**: +/-4% of the rated tape velocity
- **Rewind time**: 60 seconds to rewind a 2400 foot reel

### Drive and Formatter

| Parameter   | Specs               |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Voltage     | 120 VAC, 22 Amps    |
|             | 220 VAC, 12 Amps    |

### Drive

| Parameter   | Specs               |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Voltage     | 120 Vac, 15 Amps    |
|             | 220 Vac, 7.5 Amps   |

## Physical Requirements

- **Height**: 1687 mm
- **Width**: 600 mm
- **Depth**: 910 mm
- **Weight**: Approx. 150 kg

## Environmental Requirements

The following requirements apply to the magnetic tape unit only. The environmental requirements for the magnetic tape media is given in ANSI X3.40-1976.

### Temperature

| Condition  | Range                      |
|------------|----------------------------|
| Operating  | +16°C to +31°C (+60°F to +90°F) |
| Storage    | +2°C to +50°C (+36°F to +122°F) |
| Shipping   | -40°C to +70°C (-40°F to +158°F) |
| Optimum    | +16°C to +22°C (+60°F to +72°F) |

### Relative Humidity

| Condition  | Range                      |
|------------|----------------------------|
| Operating  | 20 to 80%, non-condensing  |
| Storage    | 10 to 90%, non-condensing  |
| Shipping   | Any, non-condensing        |
| Optimum    | 37 to 42%, non-condensing  |

### Duration

- **Storage**: The storage environment must not be outside the limits of the operating environment for a period longer than 6 months
- **Shipping**: The shipping environment must not be outside the limits of the storage environment for a period longer than 72 hours

### Thermal Change

The magnetic tape unit must not be subject to a temperature change greater than 16°C (27°F) per hour, non-operating or 5°C (9°F) per hour operating.
