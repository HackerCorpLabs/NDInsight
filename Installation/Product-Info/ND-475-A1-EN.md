# ND 475 Matrix Printer, GENICOM 3024

## Introduction

The ND 475 is ideally suited to provide hard copy output for small business and personal computer systems. The printer emulates the most widely-used professional and personal computer printers and provides many other standard features and options. These include operator-selectable print styles, tractor-feed capability and five different print modes. In addition, high resolution, dot addressable graphics are a standard feature, providing added capabilities for professional system requirements.

## Features

- Up to Five Selectable Print Modes (*)
- Up to Four Character Styles (*)
- Five Character Pitches
- Fifteen National Character Sets (*)
- Dot-Addressable Graphics
- Flexible Paper Handling
- Operator Convenience
- Universal Power Supply

# Technical Specifications

## Character Formation
- **Draft:** 5 of 9 dots H x 9 dots V
- **NLQ Gothic:** 9 dots H x 18 dots V
- **NLQ Italic:** 11 dots H x 18 dots V
- Emphasized mode doubles the dots horizontally; double strike mode doubles the dots vertically.

## Print Head
- **Type:** Nine wire impact
- **Speed:** 22 in./sec (559 mm/sec)

## Head Slew
- **Interval:** 40 msec at 6 lpi

## Line Feed Interval
- **Character Spacing:** 10, 12, and 17 cpi

## Line Spacing
- **Resolution:** 6, 8, N/72, N/144 lpi

## Print Line
- **Size:** 13.2 in. (335 mm) (132 columns @ 10 cpi)

## Graphics Mode (*)
- **H Resolution:** 60 to 204 dpi
- **V Resolution:** 72 or 144 dpi
- **Speed:** 570 dots/sec max

## Buffer Size
- **Capacity:** 2K character

## Ribbon
- **Type:** Cartridge, black inked nylon, 4 x 10⁶ character nominal life (draft printing)

## Test
- **Type:** Local self test

## Print Control
- **Horizontal Tabs:** 64
- **Vertical Tabs:** 54 (*)
- **Top of Form:** Form lengths to 22 in. (559 mm) (*)
- **Perforation Skip:** 0 or 1 in. (25.4 mm), 1 to 127 line (*)
- **Automation:** New Line, LF

(*) Emulation mode dependent

## Paper Handling
- **Paper Drive:** Adjustable push tractors at rear and upper pinch rollers for continuous forms
- **Paper Slew:** 7 in./sec (178 mm/sec)
- **Paper Width:** Continuous forms: 5.1 to 15.5 in. (130 to 394 mm)
- **Copies:** Original plus 3; max thickness: 0.018 in. (.46 mm)
- **Paper Tear Dimension:** 1.35 in. (34 mm)

## Operator Controls
- **Program Mode Select:** On Line/Mode Select
- **Line Feed/Font Select:** FF/Forms Length Select
- **TOF Set/Character per Inch:** Self Test

## Power Requirements
- **Voltage:** 105 to 129 and 198 to 264 VAC
- **Frequency:** 48/62 Hz
- **Current:** 1A max operating

## Operating Environment
- **Temperature:** + 40° to + 104° F (+ 4° to + 40° C)
- **Humidity:** 20% to 90% relative

## Dimensions
- **Height:** 5.0 inches (126 mm)
- **Width:** 24.7 inches (627 mm)
- **Depth:** 15.6 inches (396 mm)
- **Weight:** 34 lbs. (15.4 kg)
