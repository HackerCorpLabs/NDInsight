# ND 310 Floppy Disk Drive, 308 Kbyte

## INTRODUCTION

ND 310 Floppy Disk Drive is provided with:

A diskette clamping/registration design which eliminates the possibility of damage to the diskette due to misregistration.

A ceramic read/write head designed and manufactured by Shugart to provide media life exceeding 3.5 million passes/track with head life exceeding 15,000 hours, and an activity light which indicates drive in use.

## FEATURES

- Bottom and side mounting holes for ease of installation and maintenance
- Ceramic read/write head
- Side by side mounting in standard 48 cm RETMA rack
- Die cast cartridge guide and base plate
- Positive diskette clamping registration
- Flexible interface with multiplex capability

## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

The ND 310 Floppy Disk Drive consists of:

1. Read/Write and Control Electronics
2. Drive Mechanism
3. Track Positioning Mechanism
4. Read/Write Head
5. Removable Diskette

These components perform the following functions:

- Interpret and generate control signals
- Move read/write head to the selected track
- Read and write data

The Head Positioning Actuator positions the read/write head to the desired track on the diskette. The Head Load Solenoid loads the diskette against the read/write head and data may then be recorded or read from the diskette.

# SPECIFICATIONS

## Floppy Disk System
- **Model:** Shugart SA800R

## Media Storage
- **Temperature:** 10 to 50°C

## Unit Operating
- **Temperature:** 15 to 32°C

## Relative Humidity
- **Range:** 20 to 80%

## Vibration
- **Frequency:** 5 to 300 Hz
- **Amplitude:** 0.04 mm to 0.3 g

## Recording Density
- **Density:** 3200 bpi (6400 flux changes)

## Wear
- **Description:** Approximately 3.5 x 10⁶ passes/track with head in contact

## Access Time
- **Description:** Track to track: 8 ms/step plus 8 ms settling time

## Rotational Speed
- **Speed:** 360 rpm

## Average Rotational Latency
- **Latency:** 83 ms

## Head Load Time
- **Time:** 35 ms

## Average Access Time for N Steps
- **Time:** (8 · n + 8 + 83 + 35) ms = (8 · n + 126) ms

## Transfer Rate To/From Buffer
- **Rate:** 31.25 Kbytes/second

## Data Capacity

| Format          | Sectors/Track | Bytes/Sector | Bytes/Diskette |
|-----------------|---------------|--------------|----------------|
| IBM 3740 format | 26            | 128          | 256256         |
| IBM 3600 format | 15            | 256          | 296580         |
| ND format and IBM S/32 II | 8         | 512          | 315392 (156 pages total) |

All diskettes have 77 tracks.
