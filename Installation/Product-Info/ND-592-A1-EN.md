# Hardware Disk Drives

## ND 592 14 MBytes 8” Fixed Winchester Disk  
## ND 593 22 MBytes 8” Fixed Winchester Disk  

### Introduction

The ND 592/593 Eight-Inch Fixed Disk Drives are random access storage devices, using fixed sealed modules as the storage medium. They are designed for high performance and reliability and require no preventive maintenance.

ND 592/593 may be mounted vertically or horizontally in the same space as an eight-inch flexible disk drive. The digital interface is intended to be attached to a single controller capable of controlling both this disk drive as well as a flexible drive.

### Features

- Full data recovery circuitry
- Sealed disk, head and actuator chamber
- No preventive maintenance required
- LSI circuitry
- Low noise for office use
- Vertical or horizontal mounting
- Less than 350 BTU power dissipation
- Rotary voice coil actuator

# Product Description

ND 592/593 has two disks located in an environmentally sealed chamber; no unfiltered outside air is drawn into the unit. Air is recirculated within the disk/actuator chamber and passes through a filter to ensure a contamination-free environment. A rotary voice coil actuator provides rapid access and positioning accuracy.

The use of large scale integrated (LSI) circuitry and microprocessor control means that only three circuit boards are used. Phase-locked oscillator data recovery and reference clocking increases data integrity.

ND 592/593 can be configured with up to four drives in a star or daisy-chain configuration. The interface also allows the ND 592/593 to be placed in a daisy-chain configuration with flexible disk drives.
