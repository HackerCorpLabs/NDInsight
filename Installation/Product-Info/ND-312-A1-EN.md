# ND 312 Floppy Disk Drive, 1.2 Mbyte

## INTRODUCTION

ND 312 Floppy Disk Drive reads and writes in single or double density on standard diskettes and on both sides of double-sided diskettes.

It is provided with:

- A ceramic read/write head to provide media life exceeding 3.5 million passes/track and with head life exceeding 15,000 hours.

- A Fasflex Head Positioning Actuator which positions the read/write heads to the desired track on the diskette. The Head Load Solenoid loads the read/write heads against the diskette and data may then be recorded on or read from the diskette.

## FEATURES

- Single or dual density diskettes.
- Single- or dual-sided diskettes.
- Media compatible with IBM 3740 and S/32 single-sided drives as well as IBM 4964, 3600 and S/34 series two-sided units.
- Side by side mounting in standard 48 cm RETMA rack.
- Up to 1.2 Mbyte storage per diskette.
- Easy to maintain and service.
- Fast 3ms track-to-track access time.
- Programmable door lock and write protect for improved data security.
- Same physical size as ND 310.
- ND 310 I/O compatibility.

# Product Description

The ND 312 Floppy Disk Drive consists of:

1. Read/Write and Control Electronics.
2. Drive Mechanism.
3. Track Positioning Mechanism.
4. Bi-Complaint Read/Write Head.

These components perform the following functions:

- Interpret and generate control signals.
- Move read/write head to the selected track.
- Read and write data.
