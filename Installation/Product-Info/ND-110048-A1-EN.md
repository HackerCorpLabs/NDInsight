# FILESTORE CABINET

## INTRODUCTION

The ND FILESTORE cabinet system is a flexible information storage system. Each FILESTORE Cabinet may contain various combinations of disks and/or magtape according to the customer's needs.  
This allows systems with large-size disks to be configured to provide greater flexibility. Through the use of the FILESTORE system, an optimal combination of fixed and removable disks can be used as backup media. No matter which combination of storage media is chosen, a convenient backup system is available.

## FEATURES

- Increased reliability due to state-of-the-art technology
- Excellent price per megabyte for large disk configurations
- Compact and economical packaging
- Incorporates Winchester technology for large disk units
- System flexibility in terms of combining different types of storage media (removable, fixed or magtape)
- System flexibility in terms of combining different disk sizes

## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

With the FILESTORE system, you can combine various mass storage devices to suit your particular needs. Combinations of magtapes, fixed disks, and removable disks can be placed in the FILESTORE cabinet. Up to four disk drives (a total of 1800 Mb) and a magtape may be configured in a FILESTORE cabinet.

## FILESTORE CONFIGURATIONS

The FILESTORE system can accommodate any combination of the following disk and tape storage devices:

- ND 106210, 1600/3200 BPI Magtape Drive
- ND 110017, 1600/6250 BPI Magtape Drive
- ND 106130, 140 Mb Fixed Disk Drive
- ND 110041, 288 Mb Fixed Disk Drive
- ND 106150, 450 Mb Fixed Disk Drive
- ND 106170, 70 Mb Removable Cartridge Disk Drive

# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

- **Power:**
  - ND 106210, 1600/3200 BPI Magtape drive . 360 Watt
  - ND 106130, 140 Mb Fixed Disk .......... 408 Watt
  - ND 110041, 288 Mb Fixed Disk .......... 140 Watt
  - ND 106150, 450 Mb Fixed Disk .......... 250 Watt
  - ND 106170, 70 Mb Removable Cartridge Disk ................................. 200 Watt

NB! Total power requirements for the FILESTORE system will vary according to the storage media chosen.

- **Physical characteristics:**
  - Height: 172 cm
  - Depth: 96 cm
  - Width: 60 cm
  - Weight: approx. 100 to 215 kg (depending on the storage devices chosen)

# REQUIREMENTS

- ND-110/CX or ND-500/CX computer systems
- ND 106230 15 Mhz Disk Controller
- ND 105570 Magtape Controller (required only if the ND 106210 Magtape is included in the configuration)
- ND 105600 Magtape Controller (required only if the ND 110017 Magtape is included in the configuration)

FILESTORE EQUIPMENT SHOULD BE INSTALLED IN A COMPUTER-ROOM ENVIRONMENT.

Specifications for Filestore systems installed in a computer room:

- **Temperature:** 20°C to 25°C  
  Max. gradient 3°C per hour
- **Relative humidity:** 40% to 60%
- **Electric supply:** approx. 220/240 volts +10 -15%  
  Frequency 50 Hz ± 1%

For a detailed description of this environment, refer to the manual on Environmental Standard for Norsk Data Equipment ND-41.012A EN.
