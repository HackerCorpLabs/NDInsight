# COMPACT FILESTORE CABINET

## INTRODUCTION

The ND FILESTORE cabinet system is a flexible and compact information storage system. Each FILESTORE cabinet may contain various combinations of disks and/or a magtape according to the customer’s needs. This will allow systems with large-size disks to be configured in such a way as to provide greater flexibility. Through use of the FILESTORE system, an optimal combination of fixed and removable disks can be used as backup media. No matter which combinations of storage media are chosen, a convenient backup system is available.

## FEATURES

- Increased reliability due to state-of-the-art technology
- Excellent price per megabyte for large disk configurations
- Compact and economical packaging
- Incorporates Winchester technology for large disk units
- System flexibility in terms of combining different types of storage media types (removable, fixed or magtape)
- System flexibility in terms of combining different disk sizes

## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

With the FILESTORE system you can combine various mass-storage devices in order to suit your particular needs. Combinations of a magtape, fixed disks or removable disks can be placed in the FILESTORE cabinet, up to a maximum disk size of 1350 Mb (three 450 Mb Fixed Disk Drives).

The ND 106240 and ND 106241 Compact FILESTORE systems are delivered with ND-110 COMPACT systems.

## FILESTORE CONFIGURATIONS

The FILESTORE system is designed to accommodate combinations of the following disk and tape storage devices:

- ND 106210, 1600/3200 BPI Magtape Drive
- ND 106130, 140 Mb Fixed Disk Drive
- ND 110041, 288 Mb Fixed Disk Drive
- ND 106150, 450 Mb Fixed Disk Drive
- ND 106170, 70 Mb Removable Cartridge Disk Drive

## PHYSICAL CHARACTERISTICS

The COMPACT cabinet:

- Height: 67 cm
- Depth: 82 cm
- Width: 54 cm
- Weight: appx. 55 - 150 kg (depending on the storage devices chosen)

## REQUIREMENTS

- ND-110 or ND-110/CX Compact system
- ND 106320 15 Mhz Disk Controller
- 05570 Magtape Controller (required only if the ND 106210 Magtape is included in the configuration)
- FILESTORE EQUIPMENT SHOULD BE INSTALLED IN A COMPUTER-ROOM ENVIRONMENT FOR OPTIMUM PERFORMANCE.
