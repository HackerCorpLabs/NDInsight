# Hardware Printers
## ND 452 LINE PRINTER

### PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

ND 452 is the FUJITSU manufactured printer M3023D. It combines proven printer design and modern technology to provide a high quality printer which is easy to operate and maintain.

- Printing speed: With 64-character set: 1040 lines/min., with 96-character set: 750 lines/min.
- Printing technique: Type-belt, impact
- Towel ink ribbon
- Number of columns: 132 or 136, switch selectable
- Horizontal spacing: 2.54 mm (10 ch/in)
- Vertical spacing: 4.23 mm (1/6 in) or 3.18 mm (1/8 in), switchable
- Line advance time: 13 ms for single line advance
- Slew speed: 139 cm/s (55 in/s)
- Buffer capacity: One single line of 132 or 136 characters
- Form width: 127 mm to 431.8 mm (5 to 17 in)
- Form length: 177.8 mm to 355.6 mm (7 to 14 in)
- Copies: Max 5 (including original)
- Standard bands: Norwegian/Danish, Swedish/Finnish, UK, US, ASCII
- All have OCR-B font style
- Interface: Parallel Data Product
- Controller: ND 656, Plotter/Printer, DMA controller

#### Physical dimensions:
- Height: 1.100 mm (43.3 in)
- Width: 1.080 mm (42.5 in)
- Depth: 680 mm (26.8 in)
- Weight: 330 kg (728 lb)

#### Power source:
- 200/208/220/240 VAC +/- 10%, 50/60 Hz +/- 2-4%, single phase or
- 100/115/190 VAC +/- 10%, 50/60 Hz +/- 2-4%

#### Power consumption:
- Maximum 1.7 kVA, 64-character set
- 80% printing: 1.3 kVA

#### Ambient temperature (operating):
- 5°C to 40°C (41°F to 104°F)

#### Relative humidity:
- 20% to 80% RH (non-condensing)

### INTRODUCTION

The ND 452 line printer features easily interchangeable font bands of 64 or (optional) 96 characters. The maximum print throughput is 1040 lines per minute using the 64-character band. It uses continuous, fanfold, pin-feed forms in widths from 5 to 17 inches and lengths from 7 to 14 inches.

### FEATURES

- High speed printing
- Excellent print quality on up to five-part forms
- Microprocessor based auto-diagnosis and off-line testing
- Simple type-belt exchange
- Swing gate for easy replacement of paper and ink ribbons
- Switch selectable forms-length
- Powered stacker included
