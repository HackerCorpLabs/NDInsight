# Terminal Switches

|     |     |
|-----|-----|
| ND-947 | RSK-90/36A |
| ND-948 | RSK-90/36B |
| ND-949 | RSK-90/72A |
| ND-950 | RSK-90/72B |

These models are able to handle up to 72 2-way, 36 3-way or 18 4/5 way devices (max 6 connections each line). The system may be configured with a mix of different lines by mixing the switch cards.

## Requirements

- 11 module cabinet or Compact cabinet
- ND-951 - ND-970 Terminal Switch modules

**NOTE:** ND Comtec reserves the right to change specifications without notice.

## Introduction

The Terminal Switch Units are the card frame and connector array units needed in connection with switching of communication lines.

## Product Description

- **RSK-90/36A** has the card frame and connector array faced the same direction.

- **RSK-90/36B** has the card frame and connector array faced the opposite direction.

  These models are able to handle up to 36 2-way, 18 3-way or 9 4/5 way devices (max 6 connections each line). The system may be configured with a mix of different lines by mixing the switch cards.

- **RSK-90/72A** has the card frame and connector array faced the same direction.

- **RSK-90/72B** has the card frame and connector array faced the opposite direction.
