### ND 445 Multifunction Printer

- ND 445 Multifunction printer
- ND 411 Stand for ND 408/410 printer
- ND 428 Tractor feed option for ND 410
- ND 429 Double sheet feeder option for ND 408

The ND 445 printer is the latest addition to Norsk Data's NOTIS range of office automation products. It is a correspondence quality printer which may also be used to produce high-speed draft quality output. The ND 445 printer is Norsk Data's customised version of the Philips GP 300L printer. The ND 445 printer includes an acoustic housing which reduces the sound of the printer to an acceptable level.

#### FEATURES

- A NOTIS Office Printer
- Correspondence quality at 80 characters per second
- Double hopper sheet feeder with manual insertion chute as option
- Tractor feed option
- Acoustic cover
- Draft quality at 300 characters per second
- 4 standard fonts (type styles)
- Semi-graphic character set for NOTIS
- British, French, German, Norwegian, Swedish, and Danish versions
- Horizontal pitch 10, 12, and 15 characters per inch

# The Sheet-Feeder

Double sheet-feeder consists of 2 hoppers for 200 sheets each, one stacker for printed paper and a chute for manual insertion of single sheets and multi-part forms.

NOTIS itself will handle the sheet feeder by allowing the user to issue directives ordering the printer to take sheets from the main or alternative hoppers.

# Character Sets

The printer comes in British, French, German, Norwegian, Swedish and Danish versions. In addition it can print both Greek and mathematical symbols and a set of semi-graphic characters for NOTIS.

# Technical Data

- **Matrix:** 9 x 9, 18 x 25 and 18 x 50 (single pass, context dependent)
- **Paper width:** 70 mm < 400 mm
- **Ribbon:** Cassette
- **Copies:** 3-5
- **Interface:** RS 232 C (ND 750 Limited Distance Modems may be used)
- **Transmission:** 300–9600 baud
- **Dimensions:**  
  - Width = 635 mm  
  - Depth = 515 mm  
  - Height = 200 mm  
  - Weight = 24 kg
- **Electrical:** 110/220/240 Volt ac, 50/60 Hz
- **Power:** 50 Watts on standby, 180 Watts printing
- **Acoustic:** < 56dBA whilst printing
- **Environment:** 10° – 35°C, Humidity < 20 g/m³
