### Introduction

The ND 476 is a non-impact, electrophotographic page printer providing letter quality, a printing speed up to 20 pages per minute and versatile paper handling. It has the capacity of handling a high paper load, and has program-selectable input hoppers and output stackers.

Because of its speed, the ND 476 is suitable for decentralized use, working as a shared printer for clusters of workstations.

The ND 476 comes ready to install with the Print Control System. It utilizes standard Data Communication interfaces, byte parallel or bit serial.

In order to fully utilize the Elpho 20, ND recommends the addition of the 10 bin sheet sorter (ND 104770).

### Features

- multiple fonts (available simultaneously)
- different character pitches (changeable within a print line)
- different line spacings
- various character renditions
- portrait and landscape printing
- full graphics capability, including mixing mode
- remote diagnostics
