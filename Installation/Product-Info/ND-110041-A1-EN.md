## Page 2

# 288 MB 8” FIXED DISK DRIVE

## INTRODUCTION

The ND 110041 fixed disk drive is a sealed-module, 8-inch rigid disk drive that uses state-of-the-art “thin-film” head technology. The ND 110041 provides 288 Megabytes of formatted storage. The drive’s characteristics include 18 milliseconds average seek time and approximately 200 watts power consumption. A directly coupled, DC brushless drive motor and universal power supply permit worldwide use of the ND 110041 disk drive.

## FEATURES

- 18 milliseconds average seek time
- Detachable, universal power supply
- Rotary arm voice coil actuator
- Thin-film heads
- Fast SMD controller compatibility
- Automatic actuator restraint
- High-performance linear actuator
- Daisy-chain interface compatibility
- UL, CSA, VDE standards
- Fixed or variable sectoring
- Low noise level
- Filestore rack mountable
- Low maintenance cost
- High MTBF
- Phase-locked data separation
- Dual-channel capability within 8-inch footprint
- FCC Class B compliance
- Selectable address

## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

The ND 110041, Eight-inch Module Drive (EMD), is a 3600 rpm, 14.52 Mhz transfer rate, random access, fixed-media disk drive. This drive consists of six oxide-coated disks in a sealed module, thin-film head, a directly coupled DC brushless motor, universal power supply and DC cooling fan.

Fast access to data and accurate head positioning are ensured by a high-performance, balanced rotary coil actuator and a dedicated servo surface, respectively.

The logic package consists of eight custom LSI chips and three microprocessors on three printed circuit boards.

## PREREQUISITES

- ND 106320, 15 Mhz disk controller
- A Filestore cabinet, large or compact type for mounting of the disk drive.

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Capacity
- **Formatted capacity**: 288 MB

### Configuration
- **Number of disks**: 6
- **Data surfaces**: 10
- **Servo surfaces**: 1
- **Tracks per surface**: 1217

### Performance
- **Transfer rate**: 14.52 Mhz
- **Access time (track to track)**: 5 milliseconds
- **Access time (average)**: 18 milliseconds
- **Access time (maximum)**: 35 milliseconds
- **Rotation speed**: 3600 rpm
- **Average rotational latency**: 8.33 milliseconds

### Recording
- **Bits per inch**: 15,185
- **Tracks per inch**: 960
- **Recording method**: RLL 2,7

### Data Reliability
- **Recoverable read errors**: Less than 1 in 10¹⁰ bits transferred
- **Unrecoverable read errors**: Less than 1 in 10¹² bits transferred
- **Seek errors**: Less than 1 in 10⁸ bits transferred

### Power
- **AC**: 115 and 230 V / 50 or 60 Hz
- **DC**: ±5 V, -12 V, +24 V
- **Power dissipation**: Approx. 200 W

### Environment
- **Operating temperature**: 10ºC to 45ºC
- **Operating humidity**: 20% to 80%

### Physical characteristics
- **Height**: 120.65 mm
- **Width**: 216 mm
- **Length**: 373.38 mm
- **Weight**: 13 kg (16 kg with the power supply)
