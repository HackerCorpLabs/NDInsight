# Colour Plotter - HP 7550

## Introduction

For the user who needs high-quality colour graphics in quantities for reports or presentations, Norsk Data offers the ND 110261 8-pen colour plotter. ND 110261 produces multiple copies of colour graphics quickly and efficiently thanks to the built-in automatic sheet feeder for cut paper sheets. This plotter is ideally suited for providing fancy colour graphics output from the sophisticated graphic products in NOTIS, ND's range of office support products.

## Features

- Eight pens
- Two paper sizes: A3 and A4
- Printing on paper or overhead transparency
- Automatic sheet feeder
- High pen speed: 80 cm/s
- Up to 150 sheets in paper feeder tray
- Built-in automatic pen capping and damping
- Well suited for plotting of NOTIS graphic products

## Technical Specification

### Media

| Type | Size |
|------|------|
| Paper, transparency film, vellum, double matte mylar polyester film | 210 x 297 mm (ISO A4) 8 1/2 x 11 in. (ANSI A) 297 x 420 mm (ISO A3) 11 x 17 in. (ANSI B) |

### Pens

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Number    | 8, automatic pen changing |
| Type      | Paper, transparency, roller ball, liquid-ink drafting |

### Resolution

| Attribute                   | Value                      |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| Smallest addressable step size | 0.025 mm (0.001 in.)     |
| Mechanical                  | 0.006 mm (0.00025 in.)     |

### Repeatability

- With a given pen: 0.1 mm (0.004 in.)

### Pen Velocity

| State      | Speed                                   |
|------------|-----------------------------------------|
| PEN DOWN   | Maximum: 80 cm/s (31.5 in./s)           |
| From the program | 1 to 80 cm/s (0.4 to 31.5 in./s) in increments of 1 cm/s |
| From the front panel | 10 to 80 cm/s (4 to 31.5 in/s) in increments of 10 cm/s |
| PEN UP     | 80 cm/s (31.5 in./s)                    |

## Acceleration
- **Maximum:** Approximately 6 g's
- **From program:** Approximately 1 to 6 g's in increments of 1 g

## Pen Force
- **From the program or front panel:** 15 to 66 grams

## Maximum Plotting Area
- **Pen axis:** 
  - 254 mm (9.97 in.) for A/B
  - 272 mm (10.65 in.) for A4/A3
- **Paper axis:** 
  - 411 mm (16.12 in.) for B
  - 399 mm (15.65 in.) for A3
  - 196 mm (7.68 in.) for A
  - 190 mm (7.45 in.) for A4

## Interfaces
- **RS-232-C/CCITT V.24:** Asynchronous serial ASCII with front-panel selectable baud rates of 75, 110, 150, 200, 300, 600, 1200, 2400, 4800, and 9600. Default value = 2400.

## Power Requirements
- **Source:** 100, 120, 220, 240 V, -10%, +5%
- **Frequency:** 48 - 66 Hz
- **Consumption:** 100 W maximum

## Environmental Range
- **Operating:** 0°C to 55°C
- **Non operating:** -40°C to 75°C
