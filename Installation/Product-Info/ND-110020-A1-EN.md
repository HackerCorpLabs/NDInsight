## Page 2

# LBP-8 Page Printer

## Introduction

The ND 110020 Laser Beam page printer makes use of the latest advances in digitalization and laser recording technology, and offers a vast choice of printing possibilities.

Despite its versatility, the ND 110020 laser printer is light and compact, and ideally suited as a desk-top printer in any office.

## Features

- Full NOTIS WP support, prints the full NOTIS character set, including semigraphic characters
- Takes a minimum amount of space: 47.5 cm wide, 41.5 cm deep and 29 cm high
- Noise level only 55 dB during printing
- High speed up to 8 A4 pages per minute
- 5 different fonts
- 2 download-able character sets: semigraphic and Greek-mathematical
- Printout on paper or transparency film
- Intended as a text printer only, use outside of NOTIS-WP is not supported. No graphics.

## Print Samples
