## Introduction

The ND 585, ND 586, and ND 587 are high-performance, random access storage devices with a capacity of 30, 60, or 90 Mbytes. The disks feature a removable, front insertable, top-loading cartridge of 15 Mbytes, with a fixed disk capacity of 15, 45, or 75 Mbytes.

## Features

- Quick access time
- High data transfer rate

## Additional Features

- Daisy chaining of up to 4 drives on one ND 558 or ND 559 controller
- Quiet unit (Substantial reduction of noise level)
- Unique front insertable receiver for high performance top-loading cartridge
- Address mark generation and detection
- Servo offset with early/late data strobing
- Automatic write precompensation
- Automatic write current control
- Independent, manual write protection of cartridge and/or fixed media
- Internal faulty monitoring
- Integral power supply
- Dynamic braking system

# Product Description

The disk unit consists of 15 Mbytes of removable front-load cartridge capacity, 15 (ND 585), 45 (ND 586), or 75 (ND 587) Mbytes of fixed media capacity, front control panel, cartridge receiver, spindle, AC blower system, braking system, read/write and servo heads, voice coil positioner and track-following servo, air filters, and integral power supply. A logic chassis contains read/write, microprocessor, input/output servo, and drive control electronics.

A rotation speed of 3600 revolutions per minute enables the CMD to operate at a 9.677 mHZ data rate. Head positioning is performed through the use of a field-proven closed-loop proportional servo system, with both acceleration and velocity feedback. The carriage is driven by a voice coil linear actuator, utilizing positioning information from a dedicated disk surface on the cartridge. If the fixed disk option is exercised, the user has a dedicated surface on one of the fixed disks. Field proven read/write heads provide a high degree of data integrity and servo accuracy. Dimensional integrity (necessary for the recording system) is provided for by a precision spindle which is mounted in a rigid cast aluminum deck and insures positive registration and seating of the cartridge to the spindle.
