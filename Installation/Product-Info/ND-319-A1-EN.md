# ND 319 TWIST Terminal

## Introduction

The ND 319 is a specially tailored version of the Facit Twist terminal. It is intended to be used for all of Norsk Data's interactive systems, and it is supplied and sold by Norsk Data A.S.

The ND 319 is an asynchronous ASCII video terminal with a large dual display format (A4 size portrait/landscape) which satisfies state-of-the-art design criteria for a text and data processing terminal.

## Features

- A full NOTIS text and information processing terminal
- Superslim separate keyboard
- Uppercase and lowercase characters including: line drawing, histogram, numeric subscript and superscript, Greek letters and plot characters
- Insert, delete, copy, underline, move and erase functions
- Local and remote printer control, giving hard copy, terminal bypass or transaction logging
- PUSH-keys allowing commonly used words or control strings to be transmitted by pushing a single key
- A total of 102 keys
- 12 programmable keys
- Display in full A4 format or conventional landscape format
- Black-on-paperwhite characters
- Flicker-free phosphor screen
- Full screen control
- Diagnostic status line

## Product Description

While working with a printed text on the screen, the ND 319 terminal allows the operator to switch from conventional landscape format (24 lines) to full page format (72 lines). This is done simply by twisting the screen. The screen can also be tilted and lifted to suit any operator's convenience.

Flicker-free black-on-paperwhite characters on a stable background permit the operator to work longer sessions with less fatigue. The flicker-free sharp text is obtained through a refresh rate of 65 Hz, a 206 character matrix, and a directly etched face plate for glare protection.

The keyboard includes a total of 102 logically grouped keys, of which 12 are definable function keys.

The operator has access to three menus with 50 parameters when entering the set-up mode.

# Technical Specification

## Monitor

**Display Format**  
- Landscape: 24 lines  
- Portrait: 72 lines  
- Characters per line: 80  

**Character Set**  
Full uppercase and lowercase character sets are available in Norwegian, Danish, Swedish.

**Screen**  
15-inch black on white, non-glare high resolution 40 MHz video monitor.

**Cursor**  
Underline blinking and block blinking cursors are softswitch selectable.

**Refresh Rate**  
65 Hz

**Character Size**  
- Landscape: 5 x 3 mm (0.20 x 0.12 inch.)
- Portrait: 2.5 x 2 mm (0.10 x 0.08 inch.)

## Power Requirements

- Consumption: 70 W
- Voltage: 98 to 132 V or 195 to 264 V
- Frequencies: 47-63 Hz

## Environmental Conditions

- Temperature: +10 to +40 degrees Celsius
- Relative Humidity: 20 to 85 %

## Dimensions

**Display Unit**  
- Width: front 350 mm (13.8 inches), rear 390 mm (15.3 inches)  
- Depth: 350 mm (13.8 inches)  
- Height: 440 mm (17.3 inches)  

**Keyboard Unit**  
- Width: 310 mm (12.2 inches)  
- Depth: 195 mm (7.7 inches)  
- Height: front 16 mm (0.6 inches), rear 34 mm or 44 mm (1.3 or 1.7 inches)

**Total Weight**  
18.5 kg (40.8 lbs)

## Interfaces

- V.24 / RS-232-C (I/O port)  
- V.24 / RS-232-C (Printer port)  
- 20 mA current loop through adaptor  

Transmission speed is switch selectable from 75 to 19200 baud.

## Protocols

**Line Protocols**  
- XON/XOFF  
- READY/BUSY  
- No protocol  

**Command Protocols**  
- ANSI X3.64  
- Custom
