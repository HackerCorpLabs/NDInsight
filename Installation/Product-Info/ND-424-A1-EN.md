# ND 424 Matrix Printer, Epson MX-80

## Introduction

ND 424 is a nice-looking matrix printer which fits well into the modern office environment. It is especially suitable for the many tasks within this environment.

ND 424 is simple to master, is light, elegant, easy to operate and very flexible. It may be connected to the display terminal ND 246 Tandberg TDV 2200/9.

## Features

- Easy to load and feed paper
- Ribbon cartridge
- Self test
- Light and sound signals when out of paper
- Gives two or more good copies besides the original
- Intelligent bidirectional printing
- 9 by 9 dot matrix for letters
- Rubber roller for quiet operation
- 96 ASCII characters with lowercase descenders
- Graphics capability

## Product Description

The MX-80 has a 9-wire print head that prints 96 ASCII characters with lowercase descenders and 64 graphics characters on a 9 by 9 dot matrix. The print head has an estimated life of over 500,000,000 characters, and it can be easily replaced. Print speed is 80 cps (characters per second) bidirectionally, and a long-life print ribbon is contained in an easily removable cartridge.

External features include a metal paper-guide rack, manual paper-advance knob, power switch, V.24 Interface, three control pushbuttons, and four green indicator LEDs (light-emitting diodes). In addition, the MX-80 has a tractor-feed paper mechanism and can use three-ply paper (original and two carbon copies). The On-Line pushbutton toggles the printer between on- and off-line modes. The FF (form feed) and LF (line feed) pushbuttons, functional only when the printer is off-line, advance the paper by one form (i.e.: page length) and one line, respectively. The distance that the paper advances may be changed under software control.

# Specifications

## Print Method
- **Type:** Serial impact dot matrix
- **Print Rate:** 80 CPS
- **Print Direction:** Bidirectional

## Number of Pins
- **Head:** 9
- **Matrix:** 9 x 9
- **Line Spacings:** From 0.035 cm to 3.00 cm with increments of 0.035 cm

## Throughput at 10 CPI
- Logical seeking function
  - 105 LPM, 20 character line;
  - 73 LPM, 40 character line;
  - 46 LPM, 80 character line

## Printing Characteristics
- **Character Set:** Full 96-character ASCII with descenders
- **Graphics Capability:** Each needle individually addressable in Bit Image Mode
- **Printing Modes:** 
  - Standard;
  - Double (advance paper 1/206th and repeat line);
  - Emphasized (shift right and double strike);
  - Double Emphasized (Combination of above)

- **National Character Sets:** Software selectable national character sets for USA, France, Germany, UK, Denmark/Norway, Italy and Spain

## Printing Sizes

| Size                | Characters per inch | Max. characters per line |
|---------------------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| Normal              | 10                  | 80                       |
| Normal Expanded     | 5                   | 40                       |
| Compressed          | 16.5                | 132                      |
| Compressed Expanded | 8.25                | 66                       |

## Forms Handling

- **Line Feed:** Programmable length
  - 1 to 85/72 nds
- **Form Feed:** Programmable length to 127 lines
- **Horizontal Tab:** To 112 positions

## Media Handling

- **Paper Feed:** Adjustable tractor-type pin feed, friction feed
- **Paper Width Range:** 10 cm to 25 cm
- **Number of Parts:** 2
- **Paper Path:** Rear

## Interfaces

| Interface    | Description              |
|--------------|--------------------------|
| Standard     | V.24 (RS 232C)           |
| Buffer Size  | 2 Kbyte                  |

## Switches/Lights/Detectors

- **Indicators:** Power Light; Printer Ready; Paper Out; On Line
- **Switches:** Power On/Off; On Line; Form Feed; Line Feed
- **Detectors:** 
  - Internal buzzer (bell) responds to Paper Out and error conditions with a periodic 3-second tone for 30 seconds.

## Reliability

- **Print Head Life Expectancy:** 50 to 100 x 10^6 characters
- **MCBF (Excluding Print Head):** 5 million lines

## Inked Ribbon

- **Color:** Black
- **Type:** Cartridge
- **Life Expectancy:** 3 Million characters

## Environmental Conditions

- **Operating Temperature Range:** .5°C to 35°C
- **Operating Humidity:** 10 to 80% non-condensing

## Power Requirement

- **Voltage:** 115 V, 60 Hz or 220-240 V, 50 Hz
- **Current:** <1 amp
- **Power Consumption:** 100 VA maximum

## Self Test Mode

- Depressing Line Feed Switch while turning power ON engages self-test which prints all characters in ROM.

## Physical Characteristics

- **Height:** 10.7 cm
- **Width:** 37.4 cm
- **Depth:** 30.5 cm
- **Weight:** 5.5 kg
