# ND 230 PRINTER TERMINAL, 75 cps

## INTRODUCTION

The ND 230 data terminal is flexible to meet your changing needs. The ND 230 can function as an interactive terminal or as a computer console device.

The ND 230 has a full alphanumeric typewriter-like ASCII keyboard and can communicate serial, asynchronous data at rates from 110 to 600 baud.

The user-oriented ND 230 simplifies your operations with features like a three-digit, LED terminal status control panel to signal the printer's next print positioning. It reads out the terminal status and configuration parameters, or the appropriate error code when an error condition exists.

## FEATURES

- 75 characters per second optimized bi-directional printing
- 9 X 7 dot matrix character font
- 132 character positions per line
- Full ASCII 128-character set
- Standard typewriter-like full ASCII keyboard
- 3 to 15 inch adjustable wide carriage
- Paper feed from rear or bottom
- Paper/out and paper/jam indicator
- 6 or 8 lines per inch
- Auto perforation skipover
- Forms printing of an original and up to three legible copies
- N-key rollover

# Specifications

## Power Requirements

- **Voltage**: 90-130 VAC or 180-260 VAC, 47-63 Hz, single phase
- **Power**: 
  - 50 VA max., idle
  - 75 VA average printing
  - 150 VA max. executing form feed

## Physical Dimensions

- **Width**: 660.4 mm (26.0 inches)
- **Depth**: 533.4 mm (21.0 inches)
- **Height**: 209.5 mm (8.25 inches)
- **Weight**: 18.6 kg (41 pounds) excluding options

## Acoustic Noise

- **Level**: Less than 60 dB (A-weighted), measured 0.9 m (3.0 feet) directly in front under free field conditions while printing at 150 characters per second

## Environmental (Operating)

- **Temperature**: 5 to 40°C
- **Relative Humidity**: 5 to 90% (no condensation)
- **Altitude**: To 3046 m (10,000 feet)

## Printer

- **Method**: Wire matrix impact
- **Speed**: 75 characters per second
- **Pattern**: 9 x 7 dot matrix
- **Character Set**: 95 ASCII plus 33 control character graphics plus parity error symbol
- **Characters per Line**: 132 maximum
- **Character Spacing**: 10 per 25.4 mm (1 inch)
- **Line Spacing**: 6 or 8 per 25.4 mm (1 inch)
- **Paper Drive**: 2 pinfeed tractors (4 pin)
- **Paper Feed**: Rear or bottom
- **Paper Width**: 76.2 mm (3 inches) to 377.8 mm (14.875 inches)
- **Paper Type**: Continuous feed, fanfold or multipart (original plus 3 copies)
- **Ribbon**: 54.8 m (60 yards) or 36.58 m (40 yards), auto reversing
- **Line Feed Time**: 30 milliseconds
- **Paper Slew Rate**: 190.5 mm (7.5 inches) per second
- **Adjustments**: Forms width, thickness and alignment
- **Form Length**: Programmable up to 355.6 mm (14 inches) long
- **Detection**: Paper-out, carriage jam
